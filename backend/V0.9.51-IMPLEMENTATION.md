# TRISHULA V0.9.51 — Ground/Spacecraft Command Round Trip

## Purpose
Extend the V0.9.50 command boundary into an encoded bidirectional round trip: ground command uplink, spacecraft physical execution, spacecraft acknowledgement/telemetry downlink, and ground reception/telemetry ingestion.

## Design
- Ground command is serialized as a `GroundPacket` and encoded into a `SpaceLinkFrame`.
- The frame is decoded and passed through the existing command-ingestion contract.
- The existing `GroundSpacecraftCommandLink` remains the authority for spacecraft command validation and physical actuation.
- Acknowledgement and physical spacecraft telemetry are serialized into downlink frames.
- `GroundStation` validates CRC/version and sequence semantics before accepting downlink packets.
- `TelemetryIngestionPipeline` converts accepted spacecraft telemetry into ground records.

## Physical integrity
- No synthetic spacecraft state is used to claim command execution.
- Invalid commands do not advance the simulation.
- Telemetry values are derived from the post-command `SimulationEngine` spacecraft state.
- V0.9.50 and earlier functionality remains additive and unchanged.

## Validation
The dedicated campaign verifies the encoded uplink/downlink path, acknowledgement reception, invalid-command rejection, and ground telemetry reflection of physical spacecraft state.
